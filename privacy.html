Maxim Mail

Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Maxim Mail is software that sorts a person's email into folders and can save reply drafts. It is installed for each client on a server the client owns. This policy explains what it does with your data.

What Maxim Mail accesses

  • Microsoft 365 / Outlook (permission Mail.ReadWrite): reads messages in your inbox, adds categories or moves messages into folders, creates folders, and saves reply drafts. Only the mailbox you sign in with.
  • Gmail (permission gmail.modify): reads messages in your inbox, adds or removes labels, creates labels, and saves reply drafts.
  • Your email address, to show which account is signed in.

Maxim Mail never sends, forwards, trashes or deletes email, even though the Gmail permission would allow sending. The software only allows a fixed list of actions and refuses everything else.

How your data is used

  • For each new email, the sender, subject and the first part of the new text are sent to an AI sorting service (through OpenRouter) that answers only: which folder, is it personal, is it risky. For folders where you turn drafts on, the email is also sent to an AI writing service to draft a reply.
  • Senders and domains on your "never read" list are never sent to any AI service.
  • Data is used only to sort your mail and draft replies for you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models by Maxim Mail, and not shared with anyone else.

What is stored

  • On your own server only: your settings, your sign-in (so it keeps working), API keys, and a record of each decision (message id, folder, reason). The text of your email is not stored.
  • The AI services keep data according to their own policies (for example, Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days). OpenRouter: openrouter.ai/privacy.

Google user data

Maxim Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the sorting and drafting you asked for. People do not read your email, except with your permission for support, or where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Turn sorting off at any time, or undo any single move.
  • Remove access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Microsoft at myapps.microsoft.com.
  • Ask for your server's Maxim Mail data to be deleted: email the address below.
