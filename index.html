Maxim Mail

Your inbox, sorted. Nothing sent, nothing deleted.

Maxim Mail sorts an Outlook or Gmail inbox into the folders you choose: students, clients, boards, newsletters, ads. Anything it isn't sure about goes to a Needs you folder. It can also write reply drafts that wait in your Drafts folder for you to edit and send yourself.

What it never does
  • It never sends, forwards or deletes email.
  • It never stores the text of your email.
  • Senders you mark "never read" are never shown to any AI.

Maxim Mail is set up by Maxim Golubev for each client and runs on the client's own server. It is not a public sign-up service.
